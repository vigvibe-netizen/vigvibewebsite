## Notes
- Cache: images are served with a 1-hour browser cache and must-revalidate, because
  they get swapped in place under the same filename. Do not make them immutable.
- Filenames are case-sensitive on Netlify but not on macOS. Keep everything lowercase.
- Social share image is site-bg.png. Drop in a purpose-made 1200x630 and point
  SEO.astro at it when you have one.
