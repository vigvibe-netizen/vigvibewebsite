---
question: What should you look for in a graphic designer?
category: Hiring
order: 1
answer: Look for range within one discipline rather than a portfolio that spans everything thinly, work that shows the constraint it was made under, and a designer who asks about the audience before the deadline. Awards and tool lists tell you almost nothing about whether the work will land.
