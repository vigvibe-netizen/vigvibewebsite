---
question: Where does AI actually save a creative team time?
category: AI
order: 4
answer: First drafts of copy, resizing and versioning production assets, and summarizing feedback into a single revision list. It does not save time on concept, art direction, or anything requiring taste. Teams that report real gains changed a process in the same quarter — the process usually did the work.
