The Top 5 Trends report is the anchor of Loftware's annual demand-generation program. One
research report has to carry a full campaign — paid media, email, landing pages, sales
enablement, and event graphics — without being redesigned for each channel.

## What the project actually was

Not a report design. A system. The report established the trend structure, the color logic,
and the infographic style; everything downstream inherited from it. That is why the second
and third editions took a fraction of the production time of the first.

## What shipped

A 40-page report, an infographic series lifted straight out of it, banner sets sized for
paid and email, landing page layouts, and a sales one-pager. One visual language, one file
structure, one place to change a number.

## Result

15K+ leads per campaign series, and three annual editions produced on the same system.
