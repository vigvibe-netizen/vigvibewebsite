The brief was a finished slide deck. The work started earlier than that: the argument was
in the wrong order and three slides were doing one slide's job.

## Restructure first, design second

The keynote was rebuilt around a single claim per section, with the ask moved out of the
appendix and into the middle where the room was still awake. Then the visual system —
type scale, chart treatment, photo handling — was set once and applied across the track so
every session presenter inherited it.

## Result

600K video views across the session series, and a template the marketing team reused for
the following year without a designer in the loop.
