The series ran for years, which is the part that shapes the design. Each new title had to
look like the last one without being a copy of it, and it had to be producible by whoever
was on deadline that month.

So the work was a system: a cover treatment with defined type and image zones, an interior
grid, and standing treatments for charts, pull quotes, and callouts.
